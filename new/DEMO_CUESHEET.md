# Demo cue sheet (3 minutes)

## Before you press record
- [ ] Vercel shows the latest console deploy (commit 82482dd or newer)
- [ ] Sheet shared as "Anyone with the link, Viewer" (file: PhoneHub Lagos - gadget inventory)
- [ ] Flyer and video already made, flyer score noted (they take minutes)
- [ ] Incognito window, no keys, .env, Paystack dashboard or Meta logos on screen
- [ ] Old test orders and the Quillox expenses cleared
- [ ] Keys rotated (Paystack test, EAA, Instagram)
- [ ] One full rehearsal done on the real server
- [ ] Microphone allowed for the console site in the recording browser

## Opening (say first, show on screen)
"Before this event I had started building AgenticSalesRep, a platform for Nigerian small and medium businesses. What existed was the structure: the console screens and the ways to connect accounts, but nothing worked end to end. During this event I made it work, on Meta's Muse models: voice notes and photo understanding, live Paystack payments, order tracking, lead generation, and a studio that makes flyers and videos and posts them, with a quality benchmark before anything goes out."

## If something goes wrong
- Flyer scored under 90: say "The benchmark blocked this one, so it never reaches a customer," then show one that passes.
- Mic fails on camera: say nothing, type the same question instead and continue. The Playground voice step was confirmed working on 3 Nov. Allow the microphone in the browser before recording.
- Over time: cut Under the hood, then the haggle, then the voice note. Keep sheet import, Paystack, tracking.
- Chat is slow: keep talking over it, do not cut. Do not retry on camera more than once.

## Never show
Keys, tokens, .env, the Paystack dashboard, Meta logos or Jolly, Instagram or WhatsApp DMs, TikTok.
Say "Muse Spark" and "Muse Image" in plain text. Label any older screen "before the event".

## Not shown live (say in one sentence at most)
Scheduler, automatic content job, lead scoring, cold email outreach.
